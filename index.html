HOW TO PLAY

Touch: each player has a joystick (move / jump ↑ / crouch ↓) and four buttons: □ light punch · △ heavy punch · × light kick · ○ heavy kick. Both players can play at the same time.

Block: hold back when the enemy is close. Run: push the stick fully forward (or double-tap forward). Dodge: double-tap back (costs stamina).

Combos: □□× , □×, ××, →□ (lunge), ←× (sweep). The third hit of a string ending in a kick is a Finisher (30 dmg). Special: ↘ + △ (costs 28 stamina).

Keyboard (testing): P1 WASD + F G V B · P2 Arrows + J K N M.

Each round win = 5 points. Double-player matches are always exactly 3 rounds (max 15 pts).
